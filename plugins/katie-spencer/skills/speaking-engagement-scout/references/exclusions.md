# Exclusions

Critical exclusion rules for speaking opportunity scouting. Before logging ANY opportunity, verify it does not fall into an excluded category. The core principle: **the speaker never pays money.**

---

## Automatic Exclusions

The following are NOT speaking opportunities. They are services sold TO speakers. Exclude them immediately.

### 1. Speaker Training Programs

**What they are:** Courses or programs that teach people how to become better speakers.

**Detection signals:**
- "Speaker training," "speaker coaching," "presentation skills course"
- "Learn to speak," "become a keynote speaker," "speaker certification"
- "Master your stage presence," "speaker bootcamp"
- Pricing for the speaker to attend/enroll

### 2. Pay-to-Be-in-Directory Schemes

**What they are:** Directories that charge speakers a fee to be listed.

**Detection signals:**
- "Join our speaker directory for $X/month"
- "Premium listing," "featured speaker profile," "speaker membership"
- Tiered pricing for visibility levels
- "Get found by event planners" with a price tag

### 3. Pay-to-Participate Summits

**What they are:** Events where speakers pay to present, often disguised as "exposure opportunities."

**Detection signals:**
- "Speaker sponsorship package," "speaking slot sponsorship"
- "Exhibitor-speaker combo," "sponsor a session"
- Registration fee required for speakers (not just attendees)
- "Investment" language paired with speaking slots
- "Showcase your expertise for $X"

### 4. Speaker Bureaus Charging Speakers

**What they are:** Bureaus that charge speakers for representation, rather than earning commission from bookings.

**Detection signals:**
- Annual membership fees for speakers
- "Speaker representation" with upfront costs
- Listing fees, profile creation fees
- Legitimate bureaus earn commission from bookings — if a bureau charges the speaker upfront, it is a scheme

### 5. Certification Programs

**What they are:** Programs that certify people as professional speakers.

**Detection signals:**
- "Certified Speaker," "professional speaking certification"
- "CSP designation," "speaker accreditation program"
- Training + certification combo offerings
- "Add credentials to your speaking career"

### 6. Mastermind and Coaching Circles

**What they are:** Group coaching programs for aspiring or active speakers.

**Detection signals:**
- "Speaker mastermind," "speaking circle," "speaker peer group"
- Monthly/annual membership fees
- "Community of speakers," "speaker collective" with pricing
- "Invest in your speaking career"

### 7. Book-a-Stage / Rent-a-Stage

**What they are:** Services that sell stage time.

**Detection signals:**
- "Book your spot on stage," "reserve your speaking slot"
- "Stage time available for $X"
- "Guaranteed speaking opportunity" with a fee
- TEDx-style events that charge speakers (legitimate TEDx does not)

---

## Detection Heuristic

When evaluating any opportunity, ask:

**"Who is paying whom?"**

- If the EVENT ORGANIZER pays the SPEAKER (or the speaker speaks for free): Legitimate opportunity.
- If the SPEAKER pays the EVENT ORGANIZER: Exclusion. Action = "Skip - Pay to Play."
- If UNCLEAR: Classify as legitimate but note the ambiguity in the Rationale.

### Red Flag Keywords

Any of these in the context of a speaking opportunity warrant closer inspection:

- "Investment" (when referring to the speaker's cost)
- "Sponsorship package" (when tied to speaking slots)
- "Membership required"
- "Registration fee for speakers"
- "Premium placement"
- "Guaranteed exposure"
- "Speaker package" (when it has a price)
- "Apply for $X"

### Green Flag Keywords

These suggest legitimate opportunities:

- "Call for speakers" or "Call for proposals" (no fee mentioned)
- "Speaker honorarium," "speaker fee," "speakers are compensated"
- "We cover travel and accommodation"
- "Selected speakers will receive..."
- "Guest expert," "featured speaker" (in the context of podcasts or events)
- "Accepting speaker applications" (no cost to apply)

---

## Logging Excluded Opportunities

**Always log excluded opportunities** with:
- Overall Grade = Skip
- Action = "Skip - Pay to Play"
- Rationale = specific reason (e.g., "Speaker training program — charges $2,997 for 'Keynote Mastery Course'")
- Scores = 0 for all dimensions

Logging prevents the scout from re-finding and re-evaluating the same exclusion on future runs.

## Accretive Exclusions Registry

This registry is a **living list of named platforms that have been verified as pay-to-play.** It exists because heuristics catch most schemes but not all — some platforms (like media-publisher "expert councils") wrap a paid membership inside legitimate-looking branding. Once we've identified a scheme by name, we should never re-evaluate it in the future. The registry shortcuts that.

### How to use the registry

**Before scoring any opportunity**, check whether its host organization or platform appears in the registry below. If it does, immediately classify as Skip - Pay to Play with rationale "Listed in Accretive Exclusions Registry: [name + brief reason]" — no further analysis needed.

### How the registry grows (accretion)

**At the end of every run**, the main thread reviews any opportunities classified as Skip - Pay to Play during the current run. For each one, check whether its host platform/organization is already named in the registry below.

- If yes → no action needed.
- If no → append a new entry to the registry under the appropriate category, following the entry format. This makes the skill self-improving: every new pay-to-play scheme it identifies gets memorialized so future runs catch it instantly.

This is the only place in the skill where reference files are intentionally mutable. All other references are stable methodology.

### Entry format

Each entry should include:
- **Name** — the platform/organization as commonly known
- **Type** — what category of pay-to-play (publisher council, certification mill, directory scheme, etc.)
- **Pricing evidence** — the fee structure, if known (with source URL)
- **Date added** — when this entry was added to the registry
- **Notes** — anything specific about how the scheme presents itself

### Seed registry (initial entries)

These are well-known publisher "expert councils" and similar platforms that charge speakers/contributors for placement disguised as editorial recognition. Verify pricing before treating any future variant as legitimate.

  - Date added: 2026-05-06
  - Notes: Same pattern.

  - Type: Paid council membership

  - Date added: 2026-05-06
  - Notes: Same pattern.

#### Generic Watch-Outs (re-verify every time)
- **TEDx events charging speakers** — legitimate TEDx events do NOT charge speakers. If a "TEDx" event is asking for a fee, it's either an unauthorized use of the brand or a pay-to-play scheme. Always verify against the official TEDx organizer rules.
- **"Summit" or "conference" branded events held in destination resorts** — sometimes these are genuine; sometimes they're pay-to-play with travel + speaking package combined. Always verify the speaker is paid, not paying.
