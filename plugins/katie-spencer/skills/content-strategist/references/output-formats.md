# Output Formats

Every strategic asset the Content Strategist produces follows a standard format. These formats are not suggestions — the downstream skills (plan-content, write-newsletter, repurpose-content) expect specific structures and field names.

Document titles are standardized so skills can find them in Project Knowledge by name.

---

## Positioning Framework

**Document title:** `Positioning Framework — [Client Name]`

```markdown
# Positioning Framework — [Client Name]

## Why Now: [Named Phenomenon or Shift]

### Force 1: [Name]
[2-3 sentences describing the force, its impact, and what it causes]

### Force 2: [Name]
[2-3 sentences]

### Force 3: [Name] (if applicable)
[2-3 sentences]

### The Collision
[1 paragraph on how these forces interact to create the current moment. What's the compound effect?]

## Why This: Contrarian Positions

### [One-sentence contrarian belief]
**What most people believe:** [The conventional wisdom]
**What's actually true:** [The client's view + evidence]
**The damage:** [What happens when people follow the conventional approach]

### [Another contrarian belief]
[Same format]

## Why You: Proof

### [Proof Category 1 — e.g., "The Unique Stack"]
[Evidence, story, or data that supports this proof category]

### [Proof Category 2 — e.g., "Origin Story"]
[The personal story that earns the right to make these claims]

### [Proof Category 3 — e.g., "Client Transformations"]
[Specific results with before-and-after]

## Convergence

**The conviction:** [One sentence that ties all three steps together]
**The vision:** [What becomes true for clients when this positioning works]
**The name:** [If the methodology or approach has a name — optional]

---
*Last updated: [date]*
*Built with Content Strategist — refine anytime*
```
