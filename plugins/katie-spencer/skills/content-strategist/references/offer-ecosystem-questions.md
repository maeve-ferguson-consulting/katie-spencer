# Offer Ecosystem — Question Bank

Map the client's offers, how they relate, and how content tethers to each one commercially. Without this, content builds trust but never converts. With it, every piece of content has a commercial destination.

Work through 3 phases: The Offers → The Rotation → Content-Offer Tethering.

---

## Phase A: The Offers

### Opening

- "Walk me through everything you sell or offer — from free to premium. Give me the full lineup."
- "For each offer, who is it for? Not 'everyone' — the specific person at the specific moment. Who walks in the door?"

### Deepening (for each offer)

- "What's the transformation this offer delivers? Not the features, not the deliverables — the before-and-after. What's true after that wasn't true before?"
- "What's the CTA — the exact words and link someone would use to take action?"
- "How does this offer relate to the others? Is it a stepping stone, a standalone, or a companion? Does someone typically do [Offer A] before [Offer B]?"
- "What price range is this? (Not for the document — for understanding the buyer's decision weight.)"
- "What's the one objection you hear most for this offer? What stops people?"

### Pushing Past Surface

- "You said this offer delivers [outcome]. Can you be more specific? What's the measurable or visible change? What would their business look like 90 days after completing this?"
- "Is there an offer that's the natural 'first step' from your content? The lowest-friction entry point?"
- "Are there offers you're considering launching or retiring? What's the trajectory?"

---

## Phase B: The Rotation

### Opening

- "When you write content, which offer are you typically pointing to? Is it always the same one, or do you rotate?"
- "If you could set a rhythm — like a monthly or weekly rotation — which offers would be in the cycle? What order makes sense?"

### Deepening

- "Are there seasonal or launch-based offers that break the rotation? When do those happen?"
- "Do different types of content connect more naturally to different offers? Like, does a vulnerable personal piece lead better to [Offer A], while a tactical how-to leads better to [Offer B]?"
- "How many weeks in a cycle before it repeats? Is it 4 weeks? 5? Something else?"

### Pushing Past Surface

- "Not every week needs a hard sell. Some weeks are trust deposits, some are direct invitations. How do you think about the intensity arc across a month?"
- "Is there an offer that should always be available as a fallback CTA — a universal 'if nothing else, do this'?"

---

## Phase C: Content-Offer Tethering

### Opening

- "For each offer, what belief shift does someone need to make before they're ready to buy? What do they need to believe that they don't believe yet?"
- "How direct are your CTAs in content? 'Link in bio' subtle, or 'here's exactly what to do next' direct?"

### Deepening

- "What kind of content primes someone for [Offer A]? Is it a story about transformation? A contrarian take? A proof-heavy piece?"
- "Have you ever written a piece of content that directly led to someone buying? What was it about? Why did it work?"
- "Is there a content-to-offer path that you know works but haven't systematized? Let's map it."

### Pushing Past Surface

- "When you mention your offer in content, what language do you use? Show me the exact words. 'If this resonates, [CTA]' or something else?"
- "Does the CTA placement matter? End of newsletter only? Woven into the middle? Both?"

---

## What You're Building

By the end, you should have:
- Every offer listed with: name, audience, transformation, CTA, relationship to other offers
- A CTA rotation sequence with cycle length
- Content-tethering rules per offer (what kind of content leads to which offer)
- Belief shifts mapped per offer (cross-reference with Belief Shift Bank if it exists)
- CTA intensity calibration (when to be subtle vs. direct)
- Entry point identified (the lowest-friction first step for new audience members)
